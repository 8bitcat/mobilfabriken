**Description** (max 4,000)
```
Start a game console company in a garage on January 1, 1982 – and build it into an empire through every console generation until 2026.

DESIGN YOUR CONSOLES
Sixteen steps: processor, graphics, sound, memory, cartridge or disc, controller, online, saving and case. Thirty console shapes from the eighties to today, all in real 3D that you can spin around with your finger. Put your own game in the box – PANG! comes first.

STUDIOS COME KNOCKING
The better your console and the more homes it is in, the bigger the studios that want to make games for it. Say yes, negotiate the license, pay for an exclusive or turn them down. Every game appears as a small animated clip in the resolution of its era and gets a TV commercial on release day.

MAKE YOUR OWN GAMES
Hire game developers and release your own titles. Borrow developers from the studios to build faster.

RESEARCH 1982–2026
A technology tree year by year: 16-bit, CD, 3D graphics, analog sticks, DVD, online stores, HD, motion controls, handhelds, hybrids, VR and ray tracing.

GROW THE COMPANY
Hire researchers and bug fixers, keep morale up and furnish the office. Move from the garage to a small office, an office floor, a studio and a headquarters. Survive the great game crash of 1983, the CD revolution, piracy and the chip shortage, and compete with thirty rival consoles.

Pixel art, a guide named Mio who shows you the ropes, 28 goals, daily gifts and a museum of every console you have made. No accounts and no ads. The game saves on your device and works offline. Optional purchases in the shop: investors, research packs, the Gold Pack and Supporter.
```

**Notes** (App Review, English)
```
Console Empire (Konsolimperiet in Swedish) is a single-player pixel-art tycoon game in English and Swedish. You start a game console company in a garage in 1982, design consoles (shown in real-time 3D), sign game studios, release games and grow from a garage to a headquarters until 2026.

How to play: tap NEW COMPANY, type a company name and follow the in-game guide Mio. Landscape only. The language follows the device; MENU → LANGUAGE switches between English and Swedish.

In-app purchases: MENU (top right) → SHOP. Investor, Big Investor and Research Pack are consumables that add in-game cash or research points to the open company; the exact amount is shown before purchase. Gold Pack unlocks six cosmetic finishes; Supporter unlocks 16× game speed. RESTORE restores the non-consumables. Nothing is random.

The app has no accounts, no login, no ads and no analytics. The game saves to local storage on the device and runs offline (three.js is bundled). The only network use is StoreKit and an optional check for new game content on GitHub Pages; no personal data is sent. All rival companies, consoles, studios and games are invented names; no real logos or artwork are used.

Contact: hello@8bitcat.io
```
