**Notes** (App Review, English)
```
Smartphone Empire (Mobilfabriken in Swedish) is a single-player pixel-art tycoon game in English and Swedish. You start a phone company in a garage in 2007, design smartphones (shown in real-time 3D), build and launch them, research new technology year by year and grow from a garage to a headquarters.

How to play: tap NEW COMPANY, type a company name and follow the in-game guide Mio (13 steps). Landscape only. The language follows the device; MENU → LANGUAGE switches between English and Swedish.

In-app purchases: MENU (top right) → SHOP. Investor, Big Investor and Research Pack are consumables that add in-game cash or research points to the open company; the exact amount is shown before purchase. Gold Pack unlocks six cosmetic finishes; Supporter unlocks 16× game speed. RESTORE restores the non-consumables. Nothing is random.

The app has no accounts, no login, no ads and no analytics. The game saves to local storage on the device and runs offline (three.js is bundled). The only network use is StoreKit and an optional check for new game content on GitHub Pages; no personal data is sent. All rival companies and products are invented names; no real logos or artwork are used.

Contact: hello@8bitcat.io
```
