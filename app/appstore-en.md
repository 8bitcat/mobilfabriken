**Description** (max 4,000)
```
Start a game console company in a garage on January 1, 1982 – and grow it into a tech empire that lasts until today.

YOUR FIRST CONSOLE, 1982
Design your console in sixteen steps: processor, graphics, sound, memory, cartridge or disc, controller and case. Your team builds it, and you can spin it around in real 3D. Put your own game in the box – PANG! comes first – and launch it at a show where the critics give their scores.

STUDIOS COME KNOCKING
The better your console and the more you sell, the bigger the studios that want to make games for it. Say yes, negotiate the license, pay for an exclusive or turn them down. Every game appears as a small animated clip in the resolution of its era, and gets a TV commercial on release day.

THE PHONE DIVISION, 2007
Research touch screens, wireless networking and handhelds to open your phone division. Design smartphones in thirteen steps – screen, cameras, chip, battery, materials, colors and brand – followed by tablets, laptops, watches, earbuds and foldables. All in 3D.

RESEARCH, HIRE, GROW
A technology tree from 1982 to 2026 with more than 230 inventions. Hire researchers, bug fixers and game developers, keep morale up, and move from the garage to a small office, an office floor, a studio and a headquarters. Furnish it with sixty pieces of furniture.

A LIVING MARKET
Rivals release new models year after year, the holiday rush, the financial crisis and the chip shortage hit, and the magazines write about you. Sell week by week, cut prices, go viral, and watch your museum fill up.

QUICK START 2007
Just want to build phones? Start directly in 2007 with $200,000 and a garage.

Pixel art, a guide named Mio who shows you the ropes, 28 goals, daily gifts – and no accounts, no ads and no purchases. The game saves on your device and works offline.
```

**Notes** (App Review, English)
```
Smartphone Empire (Mobilfabriken in Swedish) is a single-player pixel-art tycoon game in English and Swedish. You found a games console company in a garage in 1982, design consoles (shown in real-time 3D), publish games, and from 2007 design smartphones, tablets, laptops and watches, growing from a garage to a headquarters until 2026.

How to play: tap NEW GAME, choose CONSOLE FACTORY 1982 (the full game) or QUICK START 2007 (phones only), type a company name and follow the in-game guide Mio (13 steps). Landscape only. The language follows the device; MENU → LANGUAGE switches between English and Swedish.

The app has no accounts, no login, no in-app purchases, no ads and no analytics. The game saves to local storage on the device and runs fully offline (three.js is bundled). The only network use is an optional check for new game content on GitHub Pages; no personal data is sent. All rival companies, consoles, games and studios in the game are invented names; no real logos or artwork are used.

Contact: hello@8bitcat.io
```
